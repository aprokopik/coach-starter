# One minute phone check after each publish

A thread cannot open the live dashboard, so a merged fix only counts once the athlete has seen it on their phone. After every publish, the thread posts this list (filled in for the change) and the change stays open until the athlete answers.

## The list (about one minute)

1. Open the dashboard on your phone. Pull down or reopen it once, so it loads the new files.
2. The footer at the bottom of Today reads the new version (it changes after the next morning or daytime run rebuilds the data, so a page-only change may still show the old number: then check step 3 instead).
3. Look at the one thing this change fixed, named in the post with where to find it (for example: "Today, Morning check card shows Sleep, 1 of 5").
4. Turn your phone to dark mode, look at the same spot once more.
5. Reply in the thread with one word: **Good**, or what you saw (a screenshot is best).

## What the thread does

- Posts the list with step 3 filled in, right after the publish.
- On "Good": closes the change. On anything else: treats it as a bug report and fixes the cause.
- Never marks a change done on the strength of the local harness alone (MAINTAIN.md, Publishing step 6).
