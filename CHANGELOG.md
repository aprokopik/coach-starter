# Changelog

One version number for this coach, MAJOR.MINOR.PATCH, held in `VERSION`. MAJOR: a contract changes (`coach/engine/RULES.md`, `coach/engine/schemas.json`, a coach.json row shape, a store collection the morning run reads). MINOR: a new feature. PATCH: a fix. `setup/update.py` brings a coach made from an older kit up to the latest one.

## 6.0.0 (2026-11-08)
- Dashboard: two new trail tiles in the Trail ultra group, from the 5 s detail of hilly runs (the kit maker chose "Yes, build it" on the card, Nov 8, after asking for ascent pacing in place of descents). **Trail pace at 150 bpm** (rank 2): each minute's distance turned into flat-equivalent distance by the energy cost of running on its grade (Minetti 2002), the first 10 minutes, steep descents, hiking and stops left out, scaled to 150 bpm like run pace at 150 bpm; coach bands Fast, Strong, Comfortable, Finisher, Cutoff risk, anchored on the Grindstone 18 h cutoff. **Climbing at 150 bpm** (rank 4): feet per hour on climbs of 5% or more and 3 minutes or longer, climb heart rate after the first minute, scaled to 150 bpm, running and power hiking both counted and the share hiked shown; coach bands Building, Steady, Strong, Mountain, Fast climber. Both are 28-day averages, Estimated, with no population norm. New `coach/tools/trail_effort.py`. The Trail tile's lap-based climbing line becomes the count of hilly runs (climbing has its own tile now); durability moves to rank 3. The Descending tile and the descent confidence and falls questions were dropped (the kit maker, Nov 8).
